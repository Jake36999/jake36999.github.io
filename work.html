Featured

Custom_Agent_Forge

An auditable training-data pipeline for fine-tuning on real codebases.

The problem

Generating fine-tuning data from a codebase typically offers no way to verify, after the fact, that a given training example was correctly derived rather than hallucinated.

The approach

A five-layer pipeline — ingestion, topological analysis, semantic projection, strict contract validation, synthesis — producing ChatML and QLoRA output. Every accept and reject decision is written to a SHA-256 hash-chained ledger. Circular dependencies are quarantined and logged rather than aborting the run.

The result

Run end-to-end against encode/starlette, a real production codebase. The ledger's hash chain verified intact across the full run, and all six internal integrity dimensions reported pass.
