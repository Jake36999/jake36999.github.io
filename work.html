Custom_Agent_Forge — cryptographically-audited training data

Problem: fine-tuning on a codebase usually means dumping files at an LLM and hoping the resulting examples are correct — with no way to prove after the fact that a row wasn't hallucinated.

What I built: a five-layer pipeline (ingest → topology → semantic projection → strict validation → synthesis) that produces ChatML/QLoRA training data with a SHA-256 hash-chained ledger tracing every accept/reject decision, plus cycle-quarantine handling for the circular dependencies real codebases actually have.

Proof: validated end-to-end against encode/starlette — 1,615 nodes extracted, 198 cycle-quarantined, 6,994 ledger entries at 100% verified integrity, 427 tests passing, a 1.17M-row output matrix.
