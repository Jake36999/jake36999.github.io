Engineering standard

How these systems are built

Four practices applied consistently across every repository, in five unrelated domains.

01

Verifiable audit trails

Hash-chained ledgers and SHA256-verified diffs. Every decision is traceable, not just the final output.

02

Failures stay inspectable

Bad artifacts are quarantined and logged rather than silently discarded, so the failure can be diagnosed.

03

Scoped claims

Systems are labelled at the confidence level they have earned, with written non-goals.

04

Enforced quality gates

Readiness gates that block release on unproven output — including my own. One has already caught and discarded a non-viable model rather than shipping it.

Built for AI-assisted development

Working with AI assistance means changes land fast and regressions slip in quietly. Over a thousand automated checks across these repositories are what make that speed safe — the system keeps doing what it claims, and says so loudly when it stops.
